💡 Haggling Tips: Start at ~⅓ of the asking price · Stay friendly & polite · Bundle purchases for better deals · Walking away often triggers the best offer.
⏰ Opening Hours: Most shops 10:00 AM – 7:30 PM · Near Jemaa el-Fna until 11:30 PM · Fridays slower (prayer) · Ramadan hours may shift.
💵 Conversion (approx): 1 EUR ≈ 11 MAD  ·  1 USD ≈ 10 MAD  ·  1 GBP ≈ 13 MAD
📌 Note: Prices are averages. Items deeper in the souks tend to be cheaper than stalls near Jemaa el-Fna. Vintage Berber jewelry and large home décor can exceed these ranges significantly.
